Rehman Akbar

Owner, JSRA International


My chacha taught me to thread a machine in 2004, in a one-room unit in Sialkot. I still remember the smell of fresh polyester and the first football shirts we sent out to a local club.

Over twenty years the room became a workshop, but the rule stayed the same. If I would not let my own team wear it, it does not leave the door.

  • Own cutting, printing and stitching under one roof
  • Free mockup before we start production
  • Names, numbers and crest on every piece
